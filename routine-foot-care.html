A helping hand

When caring for your feet isn't so simple.

Getting down to your feet can be the hardest part. Limited flexibility, back or joint discomfort, reduced vision, or difficulty handling nail clippers can make a small task feel impossible. You don't have to struggle through it.

i.You can't comfortably reach your feet or bend down for long
ii.Your eyesight makes it difficult to see the nail edges clearly
iii.Thick nails or hard calluses are difficult to manage at home
iv.You want regular professional care to help keep your feet comfortable

What to expect

Everyday care, with a medical eye.

For seniors and adults of any age. We'll look at your skin and nails, listen to what bothers you, and tailor your care to your feet.

At your visit

Nail & callus care

Professional trimming and care for nails that are long, thick, or difficult to manage, plus careful reduction of calluses when appropriate.

  • Comfortable nail trimming and smoothing
  • Attention to pressure points and troublesome nail edges
  • Advice on footwear and caring for your skin between visits
Over time

A routine that works for you

We'll agree on how often you should come back based on nail growth, callus buildup, comfort, and your medical needs.

  • Regular checks for changes you may not see yourself
  • A familiar place to ask questions
  • A care plan that adjusts as your needs change

Small problems deserve attention

Comfort now. Care for what comes next.

Overgrown nails and calluses can put pressure on your toes and make walking uncomfortable. Professional care can help reduce discomfort and address nail edges before they become more troublesome.

When reaching or seeing your feet is difficult, small cuts, sore areas, or signs of infection can be easy to miss. Regular visits give us a chance to notice changes and address them early. If a nail is already ingrown or infected, we'll discuss the treatment it needs.

Yes. Routine nail and callus care is a welcome reason to book. You don't need a dramatic problem, and you don't need to be embarrassed about needing help.

Is this only for seniors?+

No. We welcome adults of any age who find foot care difficult or prefer regular professional care.

Can someone come with me?+

Of course. A family member or caregiver is welcome to accompany you and help discuss your care.
