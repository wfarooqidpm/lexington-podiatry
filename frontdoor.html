Policies & consent

The important parts should be clear—not buried in pages of fine print.

Appointment confirmation

Completing Front Door prepares your appointment but does not itself confirm attendance. Lexington Podiatry generally asks patients to confirm their appointment on the evening before the visit. An appointment that remains unconfirmed may be released so the time can be offered to another patient.

No-show policy

$50

Once you confirm your appointment, a missed appointment without notice carries a $50 no-show fee. We send a reminder at approximately 6:00 PM the evening before your visit, and you may reschedule when you receive that reminder if needed.

Booked through Zocdoc?

If you decide not to keep this appointment, please cancel it directly through Zocdoc so the booking is properly released.
