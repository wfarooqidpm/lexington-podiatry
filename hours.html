Seeing the podiatrist around your workday

  • Before work: Tuesday opens at 8:00 AM.
  • After work: Monday runs until 6:00 PM and Thursday until 7:00 PM.
  • Without leaving your desk: on Friday you can choose an in-person visit or a video visit.

Same-week appointments are usually available. The booking page shows open times before you enter any details, and the times update as appointments are taken. If you need help finding a time, call (212) 510-8665.

Getting here from Grand Central

The office is at 353 Lexington Avenue, Room 1502, on the 15th floor at the corner of 40th Street. From Grand Central, exit at 42nd & Lexington and walk two blocks south. See office directions for more.

Insurance

Lexington Podiatry is in network with Medicare, Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, 1199SEIU and TRICARE. UnitedHealthcare PPO plans are accepted out of network, and self-pay rates are available. Please confirm your own plan's coverage before your visit.
