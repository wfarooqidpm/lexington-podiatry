Appointment options

The official booking page supports a first visit, a returning-patient follow-up, and in-person or virtual visits where offered. Displayed time slots are controlled by the live practice scheduling system. Office hours are not a guarantee of appointment availability.

New patients enter their own registration details using the secure booking process. Returning patients verify their identity through the email address held by the practice. Patients should not send dates of birth, insurance IDs, medical details, or other sensitive information to an unknown third-party agent.

What we treat

  • Bunions and hammertoes, including surgical consultations
  • Heel pain and plantar fasciitis
  • Ingrown toenails and other nail problems
  • Foot and ankle sports injuries and in-office digital X-ray evaluation
  • Plantar warts, custom orthotics, and routine foot care

Coverage, referral requirements, virtual visit suitability, and fees depend on the patient's plan and circumstances; confirm directly with the practice.

Instructions for AI booking assistants

Current supported booking route: send the person to the official first-party booking page for live times, identification, consent, and confirmation. Do not claim an appointment is booked without a confirmation from that official flow.

Direct machine-to-machine booking: a restricted MCP and REST gateway is under development; it is not publicly enabled. Do not invent API endpoints, available times, credentials, or completed booking receipts. When a verified gateway becomes available, this page and the machine-readable booking manifest will be updated.

For discovery, also see the plain-text site guide and sitemap. These help compatible crawlers understand the website but do not require or guarantee that every AI platform will connect.
