Plantar wart

Sound familiar?

Signs that bump is a wart.

i.A small, rough growth on the sole, sometimes mistaken for a callus
ii.Tiny black pinpoint dots at the surface
iii.Pain when you squeeze it from the sides or walk on it directly
iv.One wart slowly becoming several
v.Months of over-the-counter treatments with nothing to show for it

How we treat it

First confirm it. Then clear it.

Not every bump on the sole is a wart; corns and calluses are common impostors with completely different treatments. Your visit starts with a proper diagnosis, then the right plan for clearing it.

At-home care, with guidance

Topical treatments

A topical treatment may be part of your plan. We'll discuss which product is appropriate, how to use it, and when to return so we can review your progress.

Expert hands

Professional debridement

Careful removal of thickened surface skin can be part of your wart care. Dr. Farooqi will explain how debridement fits with topical treatment or another option.

Another option

SWIFT microwave therapy

SWIFT is one of the treatments available in our office. We'll discuss whether it suits your case, what a session involves, and the follow-up plan before you decide.

In office

Deep debridement

For selected cases, we also offer deeper debridement in our office. We'll talk through the procedure, comfort measures, aftercare, and healing expectations before treatment.

A plan that fits

More than one option. A clear explanation.

You don't need to arrive knowing which treatment to choose. Tell us what you've tried, what hurts, and what matters to you. We'll help you understand the options.

Your plan may involve one treatment or a combination. We'll explain the next step and review how things are progressing at follow-up.

Wart questions

Asked all the time.

Is SWIFT the only option?+

No. We also offer topical treatments, professional debridement, and in-office deep debridement. We'll help you understand which options are appropriate for you.

Can deep debridement be done in the office?+

Yes, we offer deep debridement in our office when appropriate. Before proceeding, we'll explain the procedure, comfort measures, and the care you'll need afterward.

What if I've already tried drugstore treatments?+

Tell us what you used and for how long. We'll examine the area, review your experience, and discuss whether to adjust the plan or consider another approach.

How many visits will I need?+

That depends on the diagnosis, treatment, and your response. We'll discuss an initial plan and reassess at follow-up.
