What can be evaluated at a visit?

Dr. Wiqar Farooqi provides surgical and non-surgical podiatric care. Common reasons for an appointment include heel pain and plantar fasciitis, ingrown toenails, bunions and hammertoes, sports injuries, plantar warts, and questions about custom orthotics. In-office digital X-rays are available when indicated.
